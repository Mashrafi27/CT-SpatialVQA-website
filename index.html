Abstract

Do 3D medical VLMs truly understand volumetric space — or do they rely on language priors? We introduce CT-SpatialVQA, a benchmark of 9,077 clinically grounded QA pairs from 1,601 CT volumes that demands explicit spatial reasoning. Eight state-of-the-art models average only 34% accuracy, often below random chance, revealing a critical gap between linguistic fluency and volumetric spatial understanding.

CT-SpatialVQA Benchmark

Spatially-Grounded QA Design

Comprehensive Evaluation

Method

CT-SpatialVQA pipeline. (top) QA generation: CT scans + radiology reports → Generator LLM → Validator LLM → human auditors → benchmark. (bottom) Evaluation: 3D CT + question → VLM → LLM-as-Jury + text metrics.
